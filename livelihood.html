Livelihood & Community Development

From skills to earning

Sashakt helps families use their skills, improve financial knowledge and access available services, creating opportunities for a more independent future.

Why this matters

Helping trained learners gain work experience and helping families understand money, public services and government schemes.

Who we work with

Trained tailoring and beauty learners, families, school students and community members.
