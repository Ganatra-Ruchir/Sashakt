Our approach

How we work

01

Understand needs

Community assessments help identify the support families need.

02

Connect families

Centres help people access relevant programmes and services.

03

Deliver practical activities

Community teams and partner institutions deliver learning, health and skills activities.

04

Review progress

Participation and programme outcomes are tracked to improve the work.
