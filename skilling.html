Pillar 02 · Project Sashakt

Skilling & Employment

Practical skills for a better future

Sashakt helps learners build practical skills, prepare for jobs and explore self-employment through vocational courses and technical training at partner institutes.

Why this matters

Helping people gain skills they can use to find work, start earning independently or continue their technical education.

Who we work with

Community learners, women seeking earning opportunities, and young people at technical institutes.

Our work

Programmes and activities

Self-employed tailoring

Learn stitching, garment making and finishing. The course prepares learners to take on tailoring work independently or build experience at Diva Design Studio.

Beauty therapy

Practise beauty and wellness services under guidance. Graduates can gain further work experience through Diva Salon & Academy.

Data entry and GST with Tally

Learn computer-based data entry, accuracy, basic accounting and GST tasks using Tally.
