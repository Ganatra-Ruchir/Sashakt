Project Sashakt

What is Sashakt?

A community initiative helping migrant and factory-worker families in Ahmedabad access healthcare, education, skills and earning opportunities.

A family-centred approach

Project Sashakt is run by Elixir Foundation and supported by Home First Finance Company. It began with a survey of families in Narol and grew into a community programme that connects people with essential services and opportunities to learn and earn.

Instead of treating health, education and livelihoods separately, Sashakt works with families through four connected programme pillars.

Health & Medical

Regular medical care, health camps and awareness.

Practical skills for jobs and self-employment.
