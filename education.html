Education & School Development

Learning through experience

Sashakt creates opportunities for children to learn, explore and build confidence through creative sessions, science labs, school support and educational visits.

Why this matters

Making learning more practical and enjoyable, while supporting children's confidence, curiosity, teamwork and communication.

Who we work with

Children in Bal Sashakt and students in partner schools, with Science Labs designed for grades 4–8.
