Self-employed tailoring

Learn to stitch and earn

01

Machine practice

Learn to handle sewing equipment and practise basic stitches.

02

Making garments

Work with measurements, fabric and garment construction.

03

Finishing and quality

Practise neat finishing and check completed work.

Work opportunities

Explore independent tailoring and further production experience.
